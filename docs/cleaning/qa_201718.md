## 1. Open questions for the team

1. **Preschool in the denominator (most important).** `enrollment_total = TOT_ENR_M + TOT_ENR_F`
   follows the playbook formula, but the 2017-18 data indicate that `TOT_ENR` **includes preschool**,
   while seclusion counts appear to cover **K-12 and ungraded students only**. The local documentation
   doesn't state either directly. The evidence:
   - In the 1,740 preschool-only schools, `TOT_ENR` is within 2 of the preschool enrollment `TOT_PSENR`
     on 1,499 (diagnostics D4).
   - All seclusion fields are `-9` (not applicable) on exactly the 1,908 schools with no KG-12 or
     ungraded students (1,728 of them preschool-only). This is diagnostics D7.
   - 26,365 analysis-ready schools have preschool enrollment > 0, with a median of 8.2% of `TOT_ENR`.

   So for schools with preschool, `seclusion_rate`, `*_per_100` and `pct_*` use a slightly larger
   denominator than the seclusion population. This formula was kept pending a team decision. No K-12
   denominator was built. Should the team build one (`TOT_ENR - TOT_PSENR`) for every year?
2. **New York City all-zero seclusion.** All 1,603 NYC schools (LEAID 3620580) report a valid 0 for every
   seclusion field, so they are analysis-ready with rate 0. Their `SCH_FTESECURITY_LEO` is `-6` at all
   1,603, and `SCH_FTESECURITY_GUA` is `-6` at the 1,595 non-JJ schools (the 8 JJ schools have `-9`). TD
   §7.1 p.11 says NYC's `-6` codes should have been action-plan codes. The zeros are kept as reported,
   and no column was added. Should the team treat NYC differently?
3. **Strict seclusion rule and action-plan clusters.** `seclusion_instances` is NA if any of the three
   groups is a reserved code. A `-5` code (action plan, missing) is never read as 0. This removes:
   - Philadelphia City SD (4218990): 217 schools with pattern `-5 / ok / -5`.
   - Stafford County, VA (5103660 and 5100088): 32 schools with pattern `-5 / ok / ok`.

   These schools are not analysis-ready. Is the team OK with that?
4. **JJ classification.** `jj` is kept exactly as reported (602 Yes / 97,030 No). Some JJ=No schools
   look like justice facilities:
   - 420080699999 SCI-Camp Hill (PA) and 220004701781 Southside Alternative HS, LA Office of Juvenile
     Justice, which are both FTE review-trigger schools.
   - 152 JJ=No schools whose names match justice terms (diagnostics D6).
   - The FTE review also found several juvenile detention centers coded JJ=No.

   Should the team use another justice indicator?
5. **Letter IDs.** Playbook §6 requires digit-only IDs, but 2017-18 has official IDs with letters in
   510 school rows (452 LEAs):
   - shape `99AA999` (`06CC###`): 482 schools in 445 LEAs, California, mostly charters;
   - shape `99AAA99` (`##SOP##`): 28 schools in 7 LEAs, state-operated programs in IA, MD, MA, NV, NH
     and TN.

   They are kept unchanged and not padded, and each row is logged as `non_digit_id`. Their COMBOKEY is
   12 characters and equals LEAID + SCHID. Should the playbook allow `^[0-9A-Z]{7}$` LEAIDs?
6. **FTE outliers.** 71 FTE values at 68 schools are flagged and **retained pending team review**
   (section 9). Correcting them, setting them to NA or capping them is a team decision. The review was
   not exhaustive.
7. **Subgroup above total.** IDEA enrollment exceeds total enrollment on 222 schools (by 1-3 students;
   196 of them are special-education schools). EL exceeds total on 10 schools (by 1-2, and by 37 at
   403246001801 WEWOKA HS). The corresponding `pct_*` is NA and the rows are logged. The small excesses
   are consistent with the TD's ±1 perturbation of student counts (TD §4 p.8), but this can't be proven.
8. **Documentation inconsistencies** (proposed doc corrections):
   - The audit's "must have" list includes `SCH_FTETEACH_CERT`, but the playbook's 50-column output does
     not. It was not added. Should it be?
   - The audit/dictionary labels for `-5` and `-9` are wrong for 2017-18. MAN §5.1.1 Table 2 p.12 says
     **-5 = Action Plan** and **-9 = Not Applicable/Skipped** (see section 5).
   - The audit's first pass knew only codes -9, -5, -2, -8 and -3. In the 2017-18 fields used here,
     the codes found are -5, -6, -9 and -11. All negatives are treated as reserved.
   - The playbook writes grade fields as `SCH_GRADE_G1`...`G12`; the 2017-18 headers are
     `SCH_GRADE_G01`...`G12`.
   - Playbook §2.2 says only School Characteristics needs cp1252. Three 2017-18 files do (section 3).
   - Playbook §4 assumes `TOT_ENR` excludes preschool (question 1).
   - Playbook §6 requires digit-only IDs (question 5).
   - The playbook mentions 2017-18 School Support extras (first-year/absent teacher FTEs,
     `SCH_TEACHERS_*_TOT`). They are not in the shared output.
   - The dictionary's shorter "suggested analysis-ready columns" list was ignored in favour of
     playbook §3.1.

## 9. FTE review and outlier decisions

**Method.**
1. **Review triggers** (playbook §1 step 10): teachers > 2,000, or any other core FTE > 200. These
   triggered 10 values at 10 schools.
2. **Top 10 per field:** for each of the 7 core FTE fields, the 10 largest values (70 entries) were
   reviewed. Each was compared with:
   - the school's enrollment and teacher FTE;
   - the "peer max": the largest value of the same field at the LEA's other schools;
   - OCR's flags for the LEA in the Appndx C workbook, STAF sheet. Check 84 = counselors, nurses,
     psychologists or security staff ≥ teachers (not applicable to justice facilities). Check 11,
     Check 15 and the univariate/multivariate STAF checks are other OCR flags.
3. **Groups** for values flagged as "identified as suspicious during review" (playbook §4):
   - **A:** at least 15x the peer max, or the peers are all 0. This is consistent with a x100 or
     decimal entry error (e.g. 0.50 typed as 50), but the cause is not proven.
   - **B:** exactly equal to the school's teacher FTE.
   - **C:** out of line with enrollment or teacher FTE, without a peer pattern.
   - **LEA cluster:** every school in the LEA that reports the identical value in that field.
4. **Not flagged:** justice facilities, and values whose ratio to enrollment looks plausible. The list
   is below.

**Decision for every flagged value:** *retained pending team review.* The value is kept exactly as
reported, `flag_fte_outlier = True`, and there is one `fte_outlier` row in `quality_detail`. Its
`evidence` gives the group, value, enrollment, teacher FTE, peer max and OCR checks.

  ID lengths, digits and letter IDs; COMBOKEY = LEAID + SCHID; leading zeros; no duplicate school or
  district keys; every module and LEA matched.

  bool; text columns have no missing values.
